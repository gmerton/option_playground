# 0DTE ATM Iron Fly ("Fly Catchers") — Dale Perryman

Source: `2025-02-02_J1pnBRw_kV0` — "0DTE Iron Fly Strategy: $1.5M in 2 Years Trading SPX
Options (Step-by-Step Guide)" ([watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J1pnBRw_kV0)). Guest:
Dale Perryman, a retail-turned-discretionary trader (bought his first mutual fund in 1988,
learned options in 2008, ex-tastytrade strangle seller) who now **runs a small friends-and-family
"hedge fund" managing multiple accounts** and trades this daily in a Zoom group; host: John. Sales
motive is soft but present: a Discord ("trade diff"), daily Zoom calls, and a YouTube funnel
(`@25:35`).

## Verdict

> stacked flies) · Tested: NO (0DTE = intraday, not faithfully testable)**
> A disciplined, defined-risk 0DTE program that opens an **at-the-money SPX iron fly** and then
> **chases the price all day**, adding and dropping flies (3–5 at once) so that whatever the close, a
> fly is peaked near it — harvesting theta into a 2–3pm "Power Hour." The single best thing: it is

> **"$1.5M in 2 years" has no denominator** — no starting capital, no % return, no max-drawdown
> figure, and it is comingled across friends-and-family accounts he manages — so it is not a
> separable, verifiable track record. Add that the entire edge is **unspecifiable intraday
> add/drop discretion** run by a Zoom group ("Captain's Log"), demonstrated with a single winning
> day, over a benign 2023–2025 bull. Real structure, no bankable evidence → 1.5.

## Mechanics

- **Underlying:** SPX 0DTE, chosen because it **cash-settles → no assignment risk** (`@03:22`). XSP
  is a valid mini version but "doesn't have the same liquidity" (`@03:35`).
- **Structure — iron fly:** sell a put **and** a call **at/near the money**, plus buy two long wings
  **20–50 points away** (`@02:35`). Max profit if SPX pins the short strikes at cash settlement; max
  loss on either edge. Calls the group "**fly catchers**" — the goal is to have the close land near
  one of 1–4 flies (`@00:58`).
- **Entry timing:** first fly **9:30–10:00am Central** ("roll call — who's ready to fly"), skipping
  the volatile first 90 minutes and waiting out significant news; usually **~10:00am**, placed as
  close to ATM as possible, splitting the tie by waiting for price to pick a direction (`@04:03`,
  `@04:33`).
- **Add rules (keyed to the *first* fly's credit):** `@05:34`, `@05:52`
  - Collected **< $15/contract** → next add at **±7 points** (add at 10 away); **max 3 flies**.
  - Collected **$15–19** → next add at **±10 points** (adds 15 apart); **max 4 flies**.
  - Mechanic: as price drifts, add a new ATM-ish fly at the trigger and drop the far one — always
    keeping 1–2 flies until **12:30pm Central**, then "**load up the boat**" (up to 3–5 flies) in
    the last ~2 hours (`@06:39`, `@13:06`).
- **Wing width (narrows through the day):** start **max 50 wide**, "sweet spot ~40," step down
  50 → 40 → 30, and the **last 30 minutes ~20 wide** — trading off risk vs. reward as decay
  accelerates (`@07:59`).
- **Exit / stop:** no conventional stop — **exit a fly when it reaches ~90–100% loss at expiration**
  (i.e., price has run to roughly the breakeven/short-wing line), then re-add nearer the money
  (`@07:08`).
- **The thesis:** capture the **theta decay of the last hour ("Power Hour," 2–3pm Central)**, where
  "you can double your profits… or the market could snatch it away" (`@20:16`, `@20:46`).
- **Buying power / sizing:** per-contract max loss = **wing width − credit** (e.g., 50 − 15 = 35 →
  **$3,500/contract**); budget **$12,500–$20,000 per contract-per-day** to run 3–5 flies (`@08:44`,
  `@09:28`). Allocates **20% of buying power, growing to ~30%** via reinvestment (`@18:40`).
- **Execution:** closes **all four legs together 99% of the time**; experimenting (larger accounts
  only) with buying a wing block once and trading only the shorts intraday — "more dangerous"
  (`@23:14`).
- **Self-rated risk:** **6/10** on a 1 (T-bills) to 10 (gambling) scale (`@21:46`).

## Claimed edge & returns

- **"Zero to $1.5M over ~2 years, high of ~$2M"** (started 2023; ~2-year anniversary at recording)
  (`@17:58`). **No starting capital, no % return, no drawdown depth stated** — an unlabeled equity
  curve only. Self-reported, unverifiable.
- **This strategy = ~65% of his profits**; also runs a Poor Man's Covered Call (`@18:23`).
- **First-fly credits $13–$27/contract**; example Friday ~$14 in the then-low-VIX regime (`@05:00`).
- **Worked example (Fri Jan 24, 2025):** ended in 3–4 flies, closed 6101 vs. an "ideal" 6095,
  **+$26/contract — "a small win"** (`@15:09`, `@15:24`). This is the **only day walked through, and
  it is a winner** — no losing day is demonstrated.
- **"Count on one hand" the max losses in "thousands of iron flies" over 2 years — all human error**
  (`@22:58`). A worst-case *following the rules* is an **$8,000/contract loss** (`@22:30`).
- **Comparator:** the "12% profit-target / 30% stop" style of iron fly has "a **70% win rate**" — a
  fine strategy, but "you miss the chance for the bigger wins" (`@21:31`). No win rate is claimed for
  *his own* method.

## Objective assessment (where to be skeptical)

1. **The "$1.5M in 2 years" headline is undenominated and comingled.** There is no starting capital,
   no annualized return, no max-drawdown figure — just a curve with no axis. Worse, he **manages
   multiple friends-and-family accounts as a small hedge fund** (`@02:23`), so the $1.5M may be
   aggregate AUM growth across clients, not his own return, and it explicitly includes **reinvested
   profits and a growing contract count** (which he says is *why* the swings are large, `@17:03`).
   A dollar total with no denominator is not a track record — it is a number. Compare Pulver, who at
   least gave +27%/+15% on a stated condor denominator.
2. **It is ATM 0DTE short gamma — the negative-skew trade dressed as convex.** Selling the ATM
   straddle and buying wings pins your max profit to the *exact* close and your max loss to the edges.
   He admits the tail is real: **$8k/contract bad days "will happen," "live by the sword, die by the
   sword," "significant drawdowns," peak ~$2M → $1.5M** (~25% off the high, eyeballed). The whole
   pitch — "give yourself a chance for big wins to offset larger losses" (`@24:53`) — is an admission
   that the loss distribution has a fat left side he is trying to out-run with the fly's peaked
   payoff and the daily adds.
3. **The edge, if any, is unspecifiable intraday discretion.** The add/drop triggers, the "tolerance"
   he gives each fly, the tie-breaking, the wing-narrowing, the "load the boat" timing — all are
   real-time judgment calls, run by a **Zoom group with a shared "Captain's Log"** (`@15:53`). There
   is no rule set that reproduces the results; the mechanical skeleton is not the strategy that made
   the money. This is the same un-testable-discretion cap as Time Flies, but *without* Time Flies'
   separable weekly-published record to compensate.
4. **"Few max losses" ≠ profitable.** He counts full max-losses on one hand, but a short-vol book
   bleeds through *medium* losers (a fly stopped at 90–100% of loss, re-added, stopped again on a
   trending day), not just max-losses. No win rate, no average P&L, no loss distribution is given for
   his own method — only for the *comparator* strategy (70%). One winning example day is not a sample.
5. **Chasing price with stacked flies is a martingale-flavored accumulation.** Adding a new ATM fly
   every time price runs to your trigger, up to 3–5 flies, means a strong one-directional trend day
   (exactly the 0DTE nightmare) can stop out several flies in sequence — the aggregate loss is a
   multiple of the single-fly $8k, and this is the day the equity curve's big down-legs come from.
6. **Benign regime + growing size.** The 2023–early-2025 window is a strong bull with mostly low VIX
   (he notes premium is thin because vol is low, `@05:00`) — the friendliest possible tape for 0DTE
   short-gamma pinning. The record has not been through a sustained high-vol/trending bear, and the
   dollar growth is amplified by ramping contracts *into* that favorable regime.
7. **Soft but real sales/community motive.** Discord "trade diff," daily Zoom, YouTube PMs
   (`@25:35`). Less aggressive than a course-seller, but the group is the product, and "smart people
   together > the smartest consultants" (`@16:36`) is unfalsifiable social proof.

## What's genuinely sound (the diamond)

- **Genuinely defined-risk per fly, cash-settled, no assignment, no overnight gap** — the per-fly max
  loss (width − credit) is known at entry.
- **Honest about the downside for this channel:** self-rates **6/10** (not "risk-free"), names the
  **$8k bad day** and that such days *will* happen, shows drawdowns rather than a monotonic curve, and
  states plainly it is "**not set-and-forget**… not for someone who can't watch it all day"
  (`@15:39`, `@24:09`). No "guaranteed / can't lose" language — a notable absence on this channel.
- **A coherent, rule-anchored process:** credit-keyed add distances, wing-narrowing schedule, a "stay
  on the reservation / don't go off on your own" discipline (`@19:11`), and closing all four legs
  together to avoid legging risk. The *scaffolding* is disciplined even if the fills are discretionary.

## Backtestability

- **Not faithfully testable on our stack.** This is a **0DTE, intraday, discretionary, hand-managed**
  program: 10am ATM entry, price-triggered adds/drops through the day, a "Power Hour" theta grab, and
  same-day exits at ~90–100% of loss. `silver.options_daily_v3` is **EOD-only (daily resolution)** —
  it has no intraday bars, so the entry timing, the adds, the tolerances, and the last-hour decay
  that *define* this strategy are unrepresentable. This is the **honest floor: a hard null for our
  backtester**, not a to-do.
- Even the coarsest EOD approximation (open one ATM SPX iron fly, settle at intrinsic) would test a
  *different* trade — a single un-managed 0DTE fly, missing every add/drop that is the actual method —
  and any tiny 0DTE credit gets eaten by modeled multi-leg fills (mark at mid, settle intrinsic).
- **Same family, already covered:** 0DTE defined-risk SPX premium selling — see
  `zerodte_breakeven_iron_condor.md` (MEIC), `pulver_0dte.md`, and `levitation_trades.md` for the
  identical skeptical economics (thin intraday edge vs. costs, intraday-only, benign-regime samples).
  If a sibling `iron_fly_18min.md` exists, cross-reference it as the closest analog (0DTE iron fly).
  Faithful testing would need minute/tick SPX 0DTE data (CBOE/ORATS), out of current scope.

## Open questions / next step

- What is the **denominator**? On starting capital / account NAV, what is the annualized return and
  the max drawdown depth — and is the $1.5M his own or aggregate client AUM? Unanswerable from the
  video, and it is the whole question.
- What is his own method's **win rate and loss distribution** (not the 70% comparator)? How many
  *medium* losers per month, and what does a strong trend day (multiple stacked flies stopped in
  sequence) actually cost?
- How does it perform in a **sustained high-vol / trending** stretch (not seen in the 2023–25 sample)?
- **Next step (on command only):** not worth a faithful backtest — it is intraday and discretionary,
  a hard null for `options_daily_v3`. If anything, the only tractable proxy would be a generic EOD
  0DTE-fly stub under `backtests/iron_fly_1_5m/`, clearly labeled as *not* the discretionary strategy;
  more useful is simply to file this alongside the other 0DTE reviews as another undenominated,
  discretion-driven, benign-regime pitch.
