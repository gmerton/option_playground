# Earnings Strategy (posted 2025-06-06) — video `oW6MHjzxHpU`

**Thesis.** Pre-earnings IV is systematically inflated by price-insensitive hedgers, speculators buying short-dated calls, and market makers widening for gap risk. The implied move exceeds the realized move on average, so sellers earn IV crush plus under-realized moves. It's statistical: it needs many uncorrelated events.

**Screen (post).** Announcements between today's close and tomorrow's open. Option liquidity (≥10k average daily contracts, or weeklies as a proxy); tight ATM spreads; stock price cap ~$100-150 for margin. Edge conditions: **front-expiry backwardation** (event month IV > next month); IV/RV > 1 (can be waived when the whole market is under 1); current implied move > the stock's average realized earnings move (8-12 quarters, same-quarter seasonality); history of negative long-straddle P&L on the stock.

**Model (video, 72.5k events since 2007, costs included).** Blind selling ≈ 0 mean for both straddle and calendar. Three predictors are monotone: **term-structure slope front→45d (more negative = better)**, 30d average volume (higher = better), IV30/RV30 (higher = better). Trading only when all three pass keeps ~10-12% of events: straddle +9% mean (sd 48%, worst −130%, one −9,200% outlier), **calendar +7.3% mean (sd 28%, worst −105%)**. Holding the straddle to the next day's close is a consistent LOSER (post-earnings drift).

**Trade.** Post: short wide iron butterfly (ATM straddle + 1-5Δ wings), same post-earnings expiry, entered as close to the close as possible, exited the next morning **after waiting 15-20 minutes for spreads to normalise**. Video (his own preference): ATM **call calendar with a ~30-day gap**, sized at ~10% Kelly (~6% per trade); straddle at ~30% Kelly (~2%). Premium per play 1-2% of the earnings sub-account; strategy 10-30% of capital. If gapped far past the strikes: close promptly, flatten delta (drift). If still near the strikes with IV not fully crushed: can hold an hour or two.

**Our cross-check.** Our earnings code lives in `src/lib/earnings/`. Our long-straddle playbook found earnings straddles earn ≈ nothing held to expiry. ⚠ Their entry/exit is intraday (15 minutes before the close / after the open); options_daily_v3 is end-of-day, so a faithful replication needs intraday option quotes (IBKR minute bid/ask, see intraday-quotes memory). Close→next-close is NOT a substitute (it includes the drift they show loses).
