# Forward Factors Strategy (posted 2025-10-26) — video `6ao3uXE5KhU`  ⭐ their flagship

**Thesis.** Few participants trade forward vol explicitly. Near-dated flow (hedging, events, speculation) overbids the front in stress, compressing the implied forward vol between two expiries below what the back leg later realizes. Wider back-month and time-spread markets keep the edge alive for patient limit-order traders, as do mid-liquidity names too small for vol funds. Cites the paper "Term Structure Forecasts of Volatility and Options Portfolio Returns".

**Signal.** Forward variance = (σ₂²T₂ − σ₁²T₁)/(T₂ − T₁). **FF = (σ_front − σ_fwd)/σ_fwd** on **ex-earnings IVs**. FF > 0 = backwardation (favorable for a long calendar); FF < 0 = contango (weaker). Tradeable: **FF ≥ 0.20**, and higher is better.

**Evidence (video).** Since 2007: 124k (30/60), 95k (30/90) and 88k (60/90) costed spreads, ATM call calendars and ±35Δ double calendars, with liquidity ≥10k contracts/day, per-symbol capacity caps, and a ±5 DTE / ±5Δ buffer. **Unfiltered: negative mean for every pair.** Returns rise with FF by decile; break-even cutoffs sit between 0.01 and 0.41. Tuned to ~20 trades a month (FF 0.20-0.23): mean +9% to +25% per trade, win 45-58%. Portfolio backtest filling highest-FF-first: CAGR ~20-28%, Sharpe ~1.6-2.4; **quarter Kelly** gives the best Sharpe (~2.3-2.6) at nearly the same CAGR. 60/90 is best; the double calendar has a slightly higher Sharpe; the ATM call calendar is simplest and cheapest. Avoiding earnings between entry and the back expiry performs better (per the paper).

**Trade.** Screen FF ≥ 0.20 ex-earnings, liquidity ≥10k a day, back leg <100 DTE. **Never pay more than the calculator's max debit** (a debit of a few cents is ideal). Work limit orders from the bid in 1-2 tick steps. Size 1-4% (post) or 2-8% with a 4% default (video); expect some to lose the whole debit on drift. Hold to the front expiry day and close as a spread before the bell. Early exit only if FF has mean-reverted to ≤0. **Re-center by ADDING an ATM calendar if price leaves the tent while FF stays elevated** (with per-name and per-sector caps). Close early if the short leg reaches ~0.95Δ or ex-dividend risk. Earnings cases: A (front before, back after) OK; B (both after) = also short earnings vol; C = skip.

**Example.** AES: 10 DTE / 17 DTE 14.5 calls, IV 62% / 52%, forward vol 33%, FF 0.86, debit $0.10.

**Our cross-check.** FF(30,90) = **1/fvr_put_30_90 − 1**, so FF ≥ 0.20 ⇔ fvr ≤ 0.833. That's the same backwardation regime our iron-fly gate uses (FVR < 0.80 → short front vol) and the opposite of our long-straddle gate (FVR ≥ 1.20).
