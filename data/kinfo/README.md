# KINFO KB (kinfo.com, "Transparent Traders" interviews)

KINFO is a trade-verification platform. Traders link broker accounts and Kinfo publishes their P&L and an all-time
leaderboard. The YouTube channel (host Stephen Johnson) interviews traders **from the top of that leaderboard**.
Every video carries a Kinfo subscription ad. Skeptic-default scoring like every KB here.

## Provenance stance: read this before any KINFO video

- **"Verified" = broker-linked, realised P&L.** The dollars are very likely real money made. That puts KINFO
  guests above almost every other creator in these KBs on provenance.
- **It is dollars, not a return.** Kinfo books profit on *closed* positions (Malik's own correction: his "+$700K in
  6 months" was positions held 2-3 years). No account size, CAGR or drawdown is implied by the headline number.
- **Survivorship is structural.** The guest is the maximum of a distribution whose size and shape are never shown:
  all Kinfo-linked accounts, which are themselves self-selected. A leaderboard topper proves that the tail exists,
  not that the method has expectancy. Treat shown trades as outcome-selected tails.
- **So:** score provenance separately from the method. A verified record is evidence the *trader* is skilled at
  something. It is not evidence that the *pattern he describes* is that something, or that it transfers to our
  universe.
