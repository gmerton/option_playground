on growing a small account; screen-shares IBKR. Funnels to one-on-one coaching (~25 hours, his figure).

**Skeptic mandate** (same as every creator KB here): record the claims, test what is testable against our
own data (real bid/ask + house costs), score 0–5. A claim without a track record is a hypothesis, not
evidence.

⚠ He teaches **structure selection conditional on a directional view**, and never sources the view. That
is the load-bearing input and it is the thing our own book says is unsolved, so read his strategy content
as "how to express a thesis", never as "how to find one".

Layout: `videos/<type>/<date>_<id>/` (transcript, meta, notes) · `<date>_<topic>_review.md` (claims vs
evidence + score).
