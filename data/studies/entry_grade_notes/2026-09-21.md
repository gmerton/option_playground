Distances are in ADR units (20-day average daily range). Rubric rules that matter here:

- **LONG** needs the close within ±1 ADR of the 21 EMA, no falling-EMA downtrend, not "over the 9 but under the 21",
  and **at least 0.5 ADR of room under the nearest prior swing high**.
- **SHORT** needs a trend-down (below a FALLING 9 and 21 EMA, not stretched past −1.5 ADR), exhaustion (≥ +2 ADR over
  the 21 and at a prior high) or a parabolic run.
- **OUT** is everything else. An F means the trade's direction wasn't the one the daily chart was in play for.
- ⚠ Evidence: in the 153-session alert study the long day gate did NOT rank R on either universe set
  (alert_filter_study_2026-09.md), so an F is a rubric/process miss, not a proven losing setup.
