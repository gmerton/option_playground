## What the record says (descriptive)
- 202 trades in 6 months, 190 closed. **27% winners.** Average closed winner +$105k (+4.76%), average closed
  loser -$23k (-1.91%). The dollar payoff ratio is 4.6.
- **The 10 largest closed winners equal 185% of the net closed P&L ($2.17M).** The other 180 closed trades lose money
  in aggregate. One trade, SLV in January (+$1.62M), is 75% of the closed total. Open positions were marked at
  +$1.81M at the month-ends shown.
- His October stats panel: winners held 12 days on average, losers 2 days (November: 10 days and 1 day).
- This is a fat-tail record: many small, fast losses and a handful of very large winners. Whether the winners came
  from selection, from sizing or from holding is the open question.

## Caveats
- Self-published by the trader. It shows losers and a losing month, which argues against curation, but it is not an
  audited statement. 'P&L %' is his software's figure (most likely the return on the position).
- Direction (long or short) is not in the tables. It is on his per-trade slides and is the next thing to add.
- Six months, one market regime, and a heavy tail: one trade decides the sign of the closed total.
