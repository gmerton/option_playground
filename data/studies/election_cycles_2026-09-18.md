# What actually moved after each election (2026-09-18)

Descriptive: 5 elections is not a sample. Script `run_election_cycles.py`. Windows measured from the day
AFTER the election, so they answer "what was still available once the result was known".

| election | leaders, 60 sessions after | laggards |
|---|---|---|
| 2016-11-08 R sweep | regional banks +20%, metals +18%, financials +14%, small caps +11% | TLT −3%, gold −3% |
| 2018-11-06 split (D House) | solar +11%, clean +10%, TLT +8%, gold +7% | **E&P −19%**, retail −8%, energy −6% |
| 2020-11-03 D sweep | MSTR +268%, PLTR +217%, TSLA +100%, solar +63% | TLT −5%, dollar −3%, gold −3% |
| 2022-11-08 split (R House) | MSTR +66%, retail +23%, metals +19% | **E&P −10%**, dollar −5%, energy −2% |
| 2024-11-05 R sweep | PLTR +87%, TSLA +36%, MSTR +35% | metals −13%, solar −8%, clean −8%, small caps −4% |

**1. The party heuristic mostly fails.** MSTR / PLTR / TSLA led after the Democratic sweep in 2020 *and* the
Republican sweep in 2024. Energy fell after 2018 and 2022 and did not lead after 2024. What led was high-beta
risk appetite plus the theme of the moment (crypto 2020, AI 2024).

**2. One pair does behave politically:** clean energy / solar — up after 2018 and 2020, down 8-11% after 2024.

**3. Most of the move was still available after the result.** Share of the 60-session move captured on day one
(median across vehicles): 2016 **29%** · 2018 **−14%** · 2020 **~0%** · 2022 **−19%** · 2024 **48%**. In four of
five cycles you could wait for the outcome and still catch most of it. 2024 — the one Tito traded — is the
exception, and even there half the move came later.

**⚠ Confounds larger than the signal.** The Pfizer vaccine announcement landed 2020-11-09, four days after that
election, and drove exactly the value/small-cap/retail rotation the table attributes to the result. The
2022-11-10 CPI print caused a 5%+ single-day rally inside the 2022 window. With n=5 this is description only.

**For 2026-11-03:** the base rate favours buying the consequence *after* the result rather than betting the
outcome — except that the convexity study (`event_convexity_2026-09-18.md`) shows the pre-event call purchase
had a positive 5-day median across 2020/2022/2024. Those two facts point opposite ways; the reconciliation is
that the *equity* rotation was slow while the *option* payoff was fast, so the vehicle decides which applies.
