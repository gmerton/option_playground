  loser **under two days** [48:01]; and "**remove the top five trades and I'm probably break-even**" [21:02]. That's
  our right-tail book, stated by the trader himself.
- **Losers get as much time as winners.** CoreWeave (three large-size attempts at 1-1.5% risk each, all stopped,
  then it tripled) and Pony (bought and sold "like six times that day", **-5% of the account in a day** [25:00]).
- **The setups are the ones our data rejects.** His signature entry is the **episodic pivot bought on a 5-minute
  opening-range breakout on the gap day** [47:01]. That combines two of our cleanest negatives: buying the
  catalyst day (**-0.173R, t -4.70**) and intraday entry vs the close (**-1.22pp, t -3.4**). Pyramiding is NULL.
  His edge, if real, is in selection and sizing discretion we can't code, plus a size discipline we can.
- 3/5 is for the process and the disclosure, not for the setups.

## What I would take

1. ⭐ **The Pony day as the teaching example for the same-day round-trip leak.** A +166% Money Manager champion
   lost 5% of his account in one session doing exactly what Gabe's log measures (278 cycles, -$8.3k, 19% win).
   His fix is structural: a monthly loss cap and size cuts, not a better trigger.
2. **The 5% monthly drawdown rule, specified his way** (-5 adjusted R on trades initiated in the month, 1R = 1% of
   the account, size cut as losses mount). It's the most adoptable rule in this batch. It should be judged as risk
   control, and it can be measured on the house book.
3. **His stats template** (win rate, R histogram, top-N concentration, time in losers) as the format for our own
   journal review. It's the admissible use of a trade log: conformance and execution, not selection.
4. Nothing to adopt on selection or entry. EP-day ORB and pyramiding are NULL or contradicted here.

## Not tested, could be

  month once initiated-trade R <= -5 at 1% risk, and/or halve size after N consecutive losers). Outcomes: max
  drawdown, worst month, CAGR and the right-tail months it forfeits, vs the uncapped book. Uses existing trade

  close entry vs next-day entry. ~2 hours on the DR-EP script. Low prior (arm A t -4.70).
