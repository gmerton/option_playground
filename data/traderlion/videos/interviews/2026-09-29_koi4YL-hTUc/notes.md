## The system ("Stan-SLIM" = CAN SLIM + Weinstein stage analysis → earlier entries)
1. **Launchpad** [15:00–29:00]: 10 SMA, 21 SMA, 23 EMA, 50 SMA, 65 EMA converge in a tight bunch under price, then expand
   upward — ideally where the longest declining-tops line (DTL) is breaking, and **when a whole group does it at once**.
2. **Consolidation pivots** [54:00–1:32:00]: swing highs lower in the base (35–50% off the high) as the stock goes from
   Weinstein stage 1 → 2; buy through them (or the DTL) on strength, stop at the prior pivot / MA cluster.
3. **Constructive weakness** [1:32:00–1:41:00]: tight, light-volume drift into a relevant MA (whichever the stock
   "respects" looking left); "**first pullback to the 50-day on a big leader in an early-stage base is almost always a
   buy**" [1:35:00]. Buy within 1% of support, stop 2–3% below.
- **Risk** [2:09:00]: stops "tight and logical", 2–5% ideal, **never > 7–8%**; stops judged **on the close** (max-loss
  exception intraday); **1% of account at risk** per trade from a flat start, 2–3% when well up on the year; positions
  up to 10–25% at key support. Cites O'Neal: "7 of 10" first-stage CAN SLIM breakouts never fall > 7–8% below the pivot.
- **Sells** [33:00, 2:31:00]: second close below the 21-day; after a big move sell ⅓–½ on the second close below the
  10-day; break of a steep rising-bottoms line + short MA = exit (Zanger).
- **Market** [14:00, 2:24:00]: trade only in an uptrend; "3 of 4 stocks follow the market"; market up ⅓ of the time →
  be invested ~⅓; breakouts fail in downtrends and chop. Late-stage (3rd/4th) bases more failure-prone (O'Neal study).
- Bar reading [29:00–53:00]: close above mid-range on volume = accumulation even on a red day; the textbook
  distribution-day definition ignores where the close sits in the range.

## Verdict: 2.5/5
Honest risk half (tight, close-judged, logical stops; 1% risk; "if you can't manage risk on the way in you're gambling")
that matches house policy. The setup half is hand-picked current charts (MU, SMH, ALAB, SpaceX) with no counts, and
the testable pieces we have measured — intraday strength entries, market-uptrend gating, group confirmation, selling
into extension, sizing up when winning — are contradicted or null on our data. Genuinely untested: the **launchpad**
(codable: spread of the five MAs ÷ price below a threshold, then all MAs rising and close above the DTL/cluster) and
the **first 50-day pullback in an early-stage base**. Neither is queued; both are long-side and outrank nothing on the
current short-side focus. Not tested per Gabe's current direction.
