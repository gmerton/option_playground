# Swing Trading and Coffee — Knowledge Base (stub, opened 2026-09-17)

YouTube channel `Swing Trading and Coffee` (~13k subscribers at its one-year mark, Sept 2026). Nightly "levels" videos: volume-profile nodes, trend lines, fib extensions, gap "magnets"; positions in mega-caps plus a few scanner names, expressed partly in short-dated options (SPY calls, META scalp). Runs a paid Discord (discount code in every video).

Skeptic-default, same convention as `data/ariel_hernandez/`, `data/martin_luk/`, `data/theta_profits/`: the unit of value is a testable rule; every stated level gets scored against what the chart did.

**Channel-specific cautions (first video):** winners-only recall (+383% SPY calls, +30% META scalp; losers summarized as "getting stopped out in this chop" with no numbers); no stop rule stated for any position (only "at break-even maybe I'll lighten up"); adds into open positions; "confirmation tomorrow" = one more green candle, which is not a rule; targets from fib extensions and volume nodes with no stated hit rate. The one house-compatible line: "if you miss a move, sit on your hands, the pullback will come; there is always another stock."

Layout: `videos/<date>_<id>/{transcript.txt,meta.json}`, `analysis/<date>_<topic>.md`. Capture: `yt-dlp --write-auto-subs` works (public videos).
