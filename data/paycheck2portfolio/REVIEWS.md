# Paycheck To Portfolio — Review Index

Skeptical evaluation of the single leveraged-income/FIRE **system** this channel promotes.
Unlike Theta Profits (many traders/strategies), this is **one creator (Sean), one system** — so the
index is organized by **review unit**, starting with a system overview. Conviction 0–5 (durable-path-
to-FIRE-as-claimed) · Risk 1–10 · Tested? no/partial/yes. See each `reviews/<slug>.md`.

| Review | Scope | Conviction | Risk | Tested? | One-line verdict |
|--------|-------|:---------:|:----:|:------:|------------------|
| [System Overview](reviews/system_overview.md) | Whole system | 1.5/5 | 8/10 | no | Coherent, disciplined, unusually honest (uses total-return, actually hedges with puts, names leverage as the real risk) — but the "margin arbitrage (borrow 5–8%, earn 25–75%)" is a **short-vol carry trade** measured on *distribution yield not total return* (real total return ~13–35%/yr), run at **~2:1 margin** on high-beta option-income funds (MSTY/NVDY) + 3× ETFs, with much of the "income" being return of capital. Headline returns = leveraged concentrated bull beta; **never bear-tested** (system began ~June 2024); his own math shows a ~20% market drop ≈ margin call, and his high-beta book would fall far more. Sound behavioral core, unsound risk structure. |

_Conviction is deliberately low by default — it rises only with independent evidence (a stress test
through a real bear, delevered after-tax total return vs SPY), not the persuasiveness of the pitch._
