## What the client ("Som"/"Sam") says

- In the program ~1 year, no prior options experience; "multiple seven figures" made (host's words), a +5.9% month,
  "about 4%" a month from the wheel and +1–2% a month from LEAPS.
- Early on sold 7/10/21-DTE puts, got assigned on ~95% of the portfolio (HOOD, PLTR, HIMS, COIN) in the pullback,
  sold covered calls below cost, then got burned on the rebound; lost ~$150,000 on one bear call spread held too long
  ("for not following the master").
- Now: 15–20Δ puts at 30–35 DTE (45 DTE "didn't like"), layered strikes; covered calls only at or above the
  assignment price; ≤10% per name; ≤5% in LEAPS, bought on pullbacks and averaged down 2–3 times.
- Plans to sell a large commercial real-estate portfolio and move it into this; "I can scale up to 30, 40, 50 million
  dollars easily"; "the market will go down, it's going to come back".

## Against our evidence

| Claim | Our evidence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| ~4%/month from the wheel | BCI CSP study (326 names, 8 years): single-name short puts return the stock at the same delta minus costs; filters add nothing. 2025–26 was a strong tape for HOOD/PLTR/HIMS/COIN, so the wheel's return here is levered high-beta equity exposure | **Explained by beta**, not premium |
| 30–35 DTE is the "sweet spot" | Single-name 30d implied-minus-realised premium is ABSENT (VRP panel); 30 DTE short puts look positive only through direction | No premium edge at that tenor |
| Averaging down LEAPS on dips | QQQ dip-LEAP study: buying on down days = no edge vs any day; averaging down adds size into losers | No support; raises risk |
| "Scale to $50M easily" | Liquidity was the ONLY gate that mattered for short premium (VRP stage 2); single-name option markets in HIMS-size names can't absorb that without the spread eating the premium | Implausible |
| 95% assigned in one pullback | That's the risk the wheel carries: full long stock exposure exactly when prices fall | The honest part |

Nothing here is verifiable: no broker statements, no max drawdown, no year-over-year record through a bear market (he
joined after the 2025 tariff crash), and the client is selected by the seller. The $150k lesson is framed as the
client's error, which is what a testimonial is for.

## Score: 1/5

Adds nothing testable beyond Ryan's existing wheel (2/5) and LEAPS dip-buying (1.5/5) notes. Watch-outs it does show:
