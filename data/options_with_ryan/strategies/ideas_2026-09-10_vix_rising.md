## Skeptic notes

- **The wheel contradiction, twice.** DRAM and WDC are both "primed to break out" and both carry covered calls below the targets. Either the calls get rolled (cost) or the breakout is sold at the strike. The premium headline and the breakout thesis cannot both be true for the same shares.
- **Own rules, again not followed on camera:** cash ladder (18% vs 20–25%), and the GOOGL buy against the 200-day gate. This is the KB's standing finding (`STRATEGIES.md`, cross-cutting).
- **Unverifiable inputs:** Unusual Whales GEX "put wall" levels, institutional "hedging vs selling" flow reads, the Bitcoin-moved-on-the-announcement-day anecdote. None can be checked and none change the chart.
- **Course funnel:** mentorship plug at [01:38], newsletter/Instagram at [02:00]. The "eight-figure goal" framing is the channel's usual.
