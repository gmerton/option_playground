# USIC Champions KB — United States Investing Championship winner interviews

_Created 2026-09-23. Channel: **Norm Zadeh** (YouTube `UCXBlhIVxneLNSGlbhGf_xeg`). Zadeh has run the United
States Investing Championship (USIC) since 1983, and here he interviews its winners. Format: short (20–30 min)
Q&A in a fixed order: books, software, style, best and worst trades, shorting, Bitcoin, market view, plug.
Zadeh read the guest's statements beforehand and asks about specific fills._

## Provenance stance (read before citing any number from this channel)

**The best-verified returns in any KB here. They still carry almost no information about edge.**

- ✅ **Audited.** For high performers, and always for a record, Zadeh reads the broker statements himself. For
  J Law that was an 85-page IBKR statement with IBKR's time-weighted return. That beats KINFO (realised
  dollars, no account size) and every self-reported educator. ⚠ The check is described for *high*
  performers. How other entries are checked isn't stated.
- ✅ **Size floor.** The $1M+ division excludes small-account lottery tickets. Other divisions' floors aren't
  stated in these videos.
- ⚠ **One calendar year per data point.** A contest year is one draw. Two consecutive top years (Flanders) is
  much better evidence than one (J Law), and still n = 2.
- ⚠ **Unknown denominator.** Neither video gives the number of entrants. The winner of a return-ranked contest
  is the right tail of the entrants' **variance**. The host's own base rate: "anybody that can do more than
  20 or 30% should be really happy."
- ⚠ **The contest selects for concentration, and the guests say so.** J Law: his worst trade "if it's not this
  competition, I don't think I would have done". Flanders: 58% of the book in one biotech. Both headline returns
  trace mostly to one or two names:
  - J Law's +359.3% ≈ MSTR's calendar-2024 buy-and-hold of +358.5% (PLTR +340%).
  - Flanders' 2025 ≈ ABVX's +586% phase-3 gap, pyramided.
- **Rule for this repo:** a USIC result is admissible as evidence that the *return happened*. It is **not**
  admissible as evidence for a setup or a selection rule, the same standard as `feedback_gabes_trades_are_not_evidence`.
  Score the mechanics against the ledger. Treat the P&L as provenance only.
