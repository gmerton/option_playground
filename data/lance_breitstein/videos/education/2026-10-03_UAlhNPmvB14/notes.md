**Claim.** S/R "doesn't work" (he cites academics) because it is applied without five filters; most levels are noise.
1. **In play** -- unusual volume, volatility, catalyst or exceptional setup (2026 examples: metals, oil in the Iran war, crypto's
   August range break, SPCX post-IPO).
2. **Clean level** -- obvious to any competent trader, respected before rather than traded through.
3. **Proper consolidation** at the level -- shallow, tight retrace = "tension"; trapped shorts + re-entering longs fuel the break.
4. **Prior emotional reaction** at the level + massive volume and fast push-away on the break.
5. **Multi-timeframe agreement** (intraday break aligned with the prior-day high, e.g. gold).
Not every criterion is needed; "trading is probabilistic"; backtesting every random line "should" show noise.
