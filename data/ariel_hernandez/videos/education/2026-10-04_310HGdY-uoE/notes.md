# Daily Setup vs Entry Tactic (310HGdY-uoE, public, 2026-10-04, 9.5 min) -- 2/5, no new test

**Claim.** Keep two things apart: the DAILY SETUP (flat-base breakout -- his favourite -- HTF/HT pennant,
earnings gap-up, high-volume close, low cheat, undercut & rally, MA undercut & rally, higher low, pullback into a
rising 200-day) and the ENTRY TACTIC (1/5/15/30/60-min ORB, pre-market-high break, prior-day-high break,
washout + VWAP remount, washout + 620, 1-min high through a support gap). Environment decides which setups work.
The tactic exists "so that we can establish risk"; without one you are an investor, with big size a gambler.
Example: IWM pulls into the rising 200-day -> 620 + VWAP reclaim -> bought TNA (same example as BynXTHRe9NU).

**vs our ledger.** Mostly a restatement of yesterday's "Undercut and Rally" (TEST_INDEX row, 2026-10-03).
- The setup/tactic split itself AGREES with how the house is built (layer-2 selection, then execution) -- and our
  data says the split cuts the other way from his emphasis: selection carries what edge exists, the tactic does not.
  CLOSE entry beats every intraday entry (entry study 9/17, t to -3.4); Stage A triggers = a random later minute;
  ORB9 INVERTED vs a random minute (t -8.50); UR/VWAP-reclaim NULL as a trigger (t -0.31).
- "The tactic establishes risk" is true mechanically (it gives a low to stop under) but a tight intraday stop is
  what the research rejects (stop_definitions.md; DINO 9/22); risk definition does not need an intraday tactic.
- "Environment dictates which setups work": regime-feedback / strategy-localisation say good stretches are real
  but not forecastable (persistence rho -0.01). Untestable as stated.
