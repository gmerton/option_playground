Unresolved jargon (ask Gabe / confirm from earlier videos):
- "620 setup" — RESOLVED 2026-09-07 (web): Gil Morales's (Virtue of Selfish Investing) intraday timing method. 5-minute chart, 6-period EMA and 20-period EMA on price, MACD(6,20) below. Bullish trigger = the 6 EMA crossing back above the 20 EMA with the MACD turning positive, used to enter a stock that is pulling back constructively intraday; the reverse cross is the bearish/exit signal. Ariel uses it four times here as the intraday pullback trigger ("wait for a 620 setup down at the 5-day", "give me a 620 setup, give me a reclaim of VWAP so I have a low to risk off of"), i.e. the 5-min 6/20 cross is the entry bar and its low is the stop. Chart Academy lists "the 620 setup" among his core entry tactics.
- "low cheat" — Minervini vocabulary (early entry inside a base before the pivot).
- "high volume close level" — the closing price of the highest-volume session in a window (quarter / year / ever), used as support/resistance; he explicitly discounts a merely-high-volume close vs the highest-volume-ever.
