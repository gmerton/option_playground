## ⚠ Outcome and survivorship: read before using anything here

1. **Hindsight catalogue.** His stated method of "back-testing" is an Evernote library of breakouts that worked,
   back to 1980 (2020-05-27 @00:35:10; CWT @00:16:10). A library of successes has no denominator. It can teach
   what winners looked like, never how often the look fails. The two worst retractions in our ledger came from
   the same structure: the Tito fade's 17/17 became −0.26%/day, and the UR band sweep's t 27.8 was outcome-
   conditioned.
2. **"Perfect examples."** He says it himself (2020-05-27 @00:17:14). Every chart shown is post-hoc.
3. **The green portfolio.** "All of these trades are profitable because I sell my losers" (@00:23:46). An open
   book is always survivors.
4. **His scans find the biggest past movers** (top 2% over 1–18 months). Any study that selects names by a
   *completed* move and then tests "the breakout" inside it has look-ahead. Use point-in-time ranks only.
5. **His own trade record** would be survivorship-free, but it isn't evidence here even if published. It's a
   right-tail record: ~20% of trades make all the money at a 25–35% win rate. That's the profile that needs
   thousands of trades to clear t 3, and it's regime-concentrated (his 2020 "top-3 environment").
6. **Universe gap.** His parabolic shorts and bounces live in small-cap pumps and names that later delisted.
   Our liquid panel mostly excludes both, so our nulls there mean **"not shown on survivors,"** not refuted.

### Setup 2: Earnings-gap breakout ("EP"; he doesn't use that term in these four videos)

From 2020-05-27 @00:15:25–00:17:43 and @00:20:43–00:22:14 (TDOC, DXCM, LVGO, FSLY, MELI, SE):
- **Catalyst:** an earnings report that **beats EPS and revenue** and **guides higher**. Growth names (DXCM: EPS
  +261% y/y, revenue +49%, next-year estimate +54%).
- **Gap up on big volume.** FSLY traded its full average daily volume "in the first five or ten minutes".
  IPO-base breakouts are "super powerful".
- **Breaks a long range**: multi-month to multi-year, often with a series of higher lows.
- **Entry:** early, once the volume is "obvious" (ORH logic). **Stop:** LOD. **Management:** as the breakout.
- **Codable proxy:** earnings day (AMC/BMO-aligned), gap ≥ 5–10%, first-hour volume ≥ 1× ADV (or day RVOL ≥ 3),
  close > the prior 126/252-session high; optional EPS/revenue surprise > 0.

### Setup 3: Parabolic short (and its mirror, the bounce)

From 2020-05-27 @00:00:52–00:14:39; 2020-06-04 @1:42:24–1:43:57; 2020-07-02 @0:02:17–0:03:52, @0:13:58:
- **Extension:** +200–300% (up to 1000%+) in ~3 days to 2 weeks for small caps. Large caps qualify on less
  (MRNA +500% in 6 months). Ideally "on nothing": hype, pump.
- **Timing:** **never day 1–2**. Count consecutive higher closes; the best are 3–5+ (INO: 5; the April INO: 7).
  "Let everyone get squeezed out"; never short the front side ("almost blown up several times").
- **Trigger:** a euphoric gap-up/peak day, then a break of the **opening-range low** (1-, 5- or 60-min), or a
  **VWAP fail** / first red 5-min candle, or a break of a mid/late-day multi-hour range.
- **Stop:** above the day's high or the range (MRNA: "$2 risk to make 20–25").
- **Hold:** days to weeks; add on 60-min range breaks and relative weakness vs SPY; lose the rising 20-day =
  continuation.
- **Bounce (mirror):** down 50–70% in 3–4 sessions, washout gap-down; buy the **first green 5-min candle** (stop
  = its low) or the VWAP reclaim, add at the ORH. A quick trade (sold GBTC "way early").
- **Codable proxy:** close/close[−10] ≥ 2.0 **and** ≥ 3 consecutive up closes; short the first daily close
  below the prior day's low (daily analogue) or the 5-min ORL (intraday); stop = HOD.

## ⭐ The queued "Qullamaggie partial-then-trail exit" row: does it match his words?

§10 row: *"sell ⅓–½ on day 3–5, stop to breakeven, trail 10/20-day vs our full 20 EMA exit."*

**Partly.** That wording comes from the Breitstein relay clip. His unclipped 2021 interview differs on three points
that change the test:
1. **Partial = 20–25%, "can vary a lot"** (CWT exact 00:23:39–00:23:44), not ⅓–½. The ⅓–½ is from the relay
   and his 2020 TSLA practice.
2. **The partial trigger is strength ("the first burst"), not a calendar day.** Day 3–5 is the horizon of the
   *alternative* sell-everything momentum-burst style (exact 00:20:02–00:20:31).
3. **The trail doesn't start on day 1.** "Once the 10-day catches up to my initial stop" (exact 00:42:18–00:42:29):
   stop = max(LOD or BE, MA). This is the detail that makes the earlier "fast trails lose" result
   (`close<10EMA` on the full position from entry) an unfair comparison.

Also: 10-day vs 20-day is by **stock speed** (fast/slow), and the relay's "10-day for fast, 20-day for slow" agrees.

**Proposed precise spec (pre-register before running):**
- Pool: the profit-lock pool as-is (1,968 precision-tier close-entry trades, 2019-10→2026-09) **and** the generic
  house breakout pool. The tier is NULL on freeze-forward, so don't rely on it alone.
- Arms (paired on the same trades, t clustered by entry date, cap none/20):
  - `BASE` 20-EMA close trail;
  - `Q_DAY{3,5}_P{20,33,50}`: sell p at the close of day d, stop → max(LOD, entry), trail = the SMA-n close exit
    active once SMA-n > stop, n = 10 if ADR ≥ 5% else 20;

  - `Q_NOPARTIAL` (the delayed-activation trail alone, which isolates the one untested mechanism);
