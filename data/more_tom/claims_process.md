**Why not higher.** Three reasons, and they compound.
1. **Not one number in thirteen videos.** He invokes a think tank, PhDs, a proprietary database back to 2005 and
   "60 hours a day of research," and reports zero statistics, zero samples, zero t-stats. Every claim arrives as a
   slogan. Against `feedback_price_at_real_fills` and the |t| ≥ 3 bar this is unusable as evidence regardless of
   who is saying it.
2. **Two claims are wrong in the same way the book has been burned before.** Row 11 is the SMB probability=edge
   error verbatim (already 2/5), and row 5(b)'s "risk and probability of profit are symmetric" is the same confusion
   dressed as a theorem. Row 7's "no edge, ever" is falsified by his own trade type (our one certified bucket is
   premium selling, t 6.07 / t 5.62).
3. **The internal contradictions are never addressed** because the clip format hides them: he has no edge (row 9),

   In a long-form interview that tension is visible; sliced into 3-minute clips with template titles it is invisible,

**Consequence: no claim here is attributed to Sosnoff without opening the source episode at the timestamp given.**
